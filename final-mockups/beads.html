Beads on the Arc Track

One circle does three jobs. Its top 260° is the 5-hour gauge; its bottom 100° gap carries the running-agent beads; when a session blocks it closes into a full ring and cuts itself into one part per blocked session. A second, smaller arc inside it is the 7-day gauge. Ink is exactly 16.00 × 16.00pt in a 22 × 22pt box, and the box is the same in every state.

Every vertex in the mark is a circular arc. The creature is a closed chain of seven arcs joined tangentially by straight segments — it has no corners at all, not even a rounded one, because each join is G1 continuous. 7 · Live scenario, 18s loop

0.0s idle, creature still — 2.0 work starts, 1 bead, creature wakes — 3.4/4.6 beads 2 and 3 fan out — 6.0 5 agents: track full, beads fuse — 7.6 one finishes, 3 beads return — 8.8 a session blocks: ring closes, amber, chevrons — 11.0 a second blocks: ring cuts into 2 — 13.2 both clear, beads resume — 15.8 last agent ends, creature stills — 16.6 5h quota exhausted, creature sleeps.

CSS only, no script. With prefers-reduced-motion the creature stops bobbing and the alert stops blinking; every state change still happens, and the alert state never drops below 50% opacity, so nothing about the mark depends on motion to be seen.
