Why bead, not notch. Correction 1 forces round caps, and a round cap eats 0.65pt of the gap from each side, so a notch’s visible counter is (gap − 1.30). Clearing the 2.0pt 1x floor needs a 3.30pt gap: 25.7° on the outer arc and 45.6° on the inner, which is 17.5% of the inner gauge and is visually a second mouth — and it is the same 3.4pt size as the alert ring’s segmentation gap, so one gesture would mean two things. The notch cannot be built. The bead can, because it never needs a counter: painted under the live arc at full opacity, it is invisible while quota is above half (when you already know), and it stands alone as full ink on a 26% track the moment quota drops below half. It is the only sub-2pt feature in the mark that fully survives 1x.

Parity, stated not overclaimed. The outer ink edge sits at exactly r 8.00 from (11.00, 12.30), so it lands on whole device pixels at all four cardinal crossings: y 4.300, x 3.000 and 19.000. A 1.30pt stroke cannot then put its inner edge on a whole pixel, and no fractional stroke on a circle can — I snapped what a circular stroke lets you snap and claim nothing further. Centre y 12.30 puts the ink box midpoint at 10.987, 0.013pt off the box centre.

Ink. Outer circle Ø 16.000pt — the same as Tailscale’s shipping StatusBarIcon (16.00 × 16.00) and SF Symbol circle at pointSize 16. Drawn alpha bounding box 16.000 × 13.3746pt, shorter than it is wide because the bottom is deliberately open. Item width fixed at 22.00pt in every state.
