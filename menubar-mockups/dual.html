CHANNEL

The icon is a channel with a floor and a roof. The floor is the 5-hour window — a real plot of what this window has already cost, with a dotted forecast fired off its leading edge and a hatched dry zone where that forecast hits the ceiling before the reset does. The roof is the 7-day window — a hairline whose solid part is the week spent and whose tick is where the week should be by now. Agents are the cells stacked on the floor's leading edge, because agents are the reason the floor rises. A finished agent is absorbed into the floor. A blocked session swells the floor until it fills the whole channel and cuts it in half. Nothing here is a badge bolted to anything else.

GEOMETRY carries quota

Floor height = 5-hour spent. Roof fill = 7-day spent, roof tick = 7-day pace. The clear air between them is the room you have. When the week truly binds, the roof descends into the plot and the forecast collides with it instead of the top.

THE LEADING EDGE carries activity

One cell per running agent, stacked where the floor is rising — 0 cells is a flat capped terminus, 10+ saturates to a notched bar. The forecast's slope is the burn rate, so activity and quota share one mark honestly: activity is the derivative of quota.

INK carries state

Hollow = stale, no confidence. Normal = live. Solid = a human is blocking work. One continuous axis, three states, readable with no colour at all. Colour appears in exactly one place in the whole icon: the cut, and only when you are the bottleneck.

Motion budget & the honest parts

Every animation this icon owns

  • agent starts — cell scales up from its own base, 320ms. Terminates.
  • agent finishes — cell detaches and sinks 280ms; floor steps up by its real cost; ripple along the top edge 180ms. Terminates.
  • afterglow — the new floor increment holds a bright top edge for 2.0s, then fades over 600ms. Terminates.
  • fan-out drained to zero — one gradient sweep through the floor, left to right, 700ms, exits the frame. Fires once per drain, never on a single completion. Terminates.
  • dry zone first appears — hatch slides in from the reset edge, 600ms. Fires once per crossing, not once per sample. Terminates.
  • block opens — floor swells to fill the channel, 220ms; cut opens with it. Terminates.
  • block flare — 500ms rim at t=0, then a double blink at 2min, 6min, 15min. Escalating, not repeating, and each one terminates.
  • block clears — collapses back to the floor level, 200ms. Terminates.
  • nothing else. Between events the icon is a still diagram. Idle is therefore not a look you have to learn — it is literal stillness.

What I would expect to be attacked

  • The roof's horizontal axis is the week while the floor's is this 5-hour window. Same footprint, two time bases. They rhyme (both fill left→right toward their own reset on the right edge) but it is a real seam, and it is the one thing the popover has to teach once.
  • The 7-day number is not printed. The roof gives you position to about ±1.5% and “ahead of / behind pace” instantly; the exact 18% lives in the popover. If that is unacceptable the fix is 8pt more width, not a second number stacked under the first.
  • A single real absorb moves the floor by a fraction of a point. The ripple and the afterglow are doing the perceptual work; the geometry change is honest but sub-pixel until several land. The 3× loupe above is where you can actually see it.
  • The blocked state destroys the forecast while it is up — the floor and roof survive as knockouts, the projection does not. That is deliberate (you are the bottleneck, the burn rate is meaningless) but it does mean the quota reading degrades exactly when you are staring at it.
  • At 85%+ the cells knock downward into the floor. It works, but it is a second reading rule for one rare case.
