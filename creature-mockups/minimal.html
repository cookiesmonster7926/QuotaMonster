02Silhouette test

Solid black, no interior detail, no opacity plateaus, nothing knocked out. This is the pass/fail gate, and it passes: a low tapering mass with a heavy rear, a blunt nose and a countable crest, sitting on a line. It reads as a grazing animal and as an instrument at once. The body fills 62% of its own 24×10pt bounding box. Honest note: at 1× the quills are the first thing to soften, because a 1.5pt stroke accepts one antialiased edge — they remain countable, but they are not fully black.

05Live scenario — 18s loop, honest timings

Every transition below is event-triggered and terminates. Idle motion is zero: the first two seconds and the last second of this loop are completely static, and that is what the app looks like almost all the time. The only clock-driven animation in the whole design is the blink at 13.5s, which in production runs once per 60s while blocked and hard-stops at 10 minutes.

06What changed, and what it cost

Deleted: the front view, both eyes, the eye-slit, the morph, and every interior line. The old mark was an outlined animal with a meter inside it. This one is a filled mass whose own tone is the meter, so the drawing and the data are the same object.

Redrawn: the back dropped from y3.5 to y7.5. That single change is why the old quills read as stubble — there were only 1.4pt of air above the spine. They now get 4.6–7.3pt and are countable. The head shrank to a 2.09pt blunt nose and the mass moved onto the haunch, because head-dominance is the loudest cartoonishness signal there is.

One weight: the old week gauge used a hairline against a heavy line — two stroke weights in one mark, which Apple and Material both forbid in writing. The same information is now two opacity plateaus at a single 1.5pt weight.

Known limits: agent count is exact only to six — above that the comb reports a band, because a seventh quill will not fit the 2pt counter floor in 19pt of dorsal run. The 5-hour fill resolves in roughly 4% steps once snapped to whole pixels, so the numeral, not the fill, is what separates 28 from 34. And the numeral itself is a concession: it is two characters of text in a mark, permitted only because it is the sole exact channel and it now means exactly one thing, forever.
