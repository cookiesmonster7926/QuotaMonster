The sleeping creature

Two bottom-open arcs of remaining quota around a creature that moves only while agents are running, with the agents themselves sitting in the mouth the arcs leave open. Everything below is drawn at 1pt = 1px, so a mark marked “real size” is the size it will be on the bar.

1 · Construction

The mark at 10×, with the construction circles left in

Why 96 degrees. The mouth has to hold three 2.50pt dots on a 4.50pt pitch, which is 11.50pt of dock plus clearance. A 96° opening gives a 13.38pt chord at r 9.00 and leaves 1.13pt between the outermost dot and the arc terminal. Wider and the arcs stop reading as a ring; narrower and the dots collide with the terminals.

Why the sweep starts at 228°. Each arc fills from the lower-left terminal clockwise. 264° of sweep means the halfway point is 132° along, which is exactly 0° — top dead centre. That is not a coincidence I got lucky with, it is the reason the gap is 96 and not 90 or 100.

The 50% tick is a subtraction, not an addition. The user drew two little ticks sitting just inside their arcs. There is no room: the counter between the arcs is already 1.50pt and a tick inside the inner arc would need another stroke plus two counters, about 4.50pt of the 8.25pt radius. So the tick is cut out of the arc instead — a 1.00pt notch through both strokes at top dead centre. It costs zero extra ink, it is in exactly the place the sketch puts it, and it earns something the drawn tick could not: once a window falls below 50% the arc no longer reaches the notch, so “past halfway” becomes a shape you can see rather than a length you have to judge.

Honest about the timings: 18.0s total, all of it CSS, no script. The bob is a separate 0.80s cycle so it does not have to divide into 18. The needs-input change is a 0.40s cross-fade between two drawn states, not a true path morph — SVG cannot interpolate between a 264° arc and a chevron without a script, and a menu-bar icon that needs a script to redraw is a menu-bar icon that stops redrawing when the main thread is busy. Dots pop in on a 0.26s overshoot curve. The sleeping breath is 4.4s, slow enough to read as breathing and not as a pulse.

6 · What survives 1×

Measured against the floors: 1.00pt minimum stroke, 2.00pt minimum counter at 1× (1.00pt at 2×), every edge snapped to a whole device pixel. PASS means it clears at 1×. RETINA means it is clean at 2× and degrades at 1×, and I say how.

elementsize1xwhat I did
arc stroke1.50ptPASS1.5× the 1.00pt floor; at top dead centre its edges land on y 2.00 / 3.50 and 5.00 / 6.50, whole points but for one half-point edge.
counter between arcs1.50ptRETINAUnder the measured 2.00pt 1x counter floor. At 1x it reads about 75% open, not 100%. At 2x (3 device px) it is clean. The @1x rep widens it to 2.00 by dropping the outer stroke to 2.00 and the inner to 1.00.
counter, inner arc to creature1.50ptRETINASame story, same fix in the @1x rep.
50% notch1.00ptRETINAx 10.50 to 11.50 straddles a pixel boundary at 1x, so it reads as a dimple, not a break. The @1x rep cuts a 2.00pt notch on whole points (x 10.00 to 12.00).
agent dot2.50pt dPASSWell over the floor; centre y 18.25 puts its edges on 17.00 and 19.50, both whole points.
gap between dots2.00ptPASSExactly the measured 1x counter floor. This is why the dot dropped from 3.00 to 2.50.
gap between dots, compressed1.30ptRETINAOnly above 6 agents. At 1x a crowded dock reads as a dashed bar. That is acceptable: past six the number stops mattering and the texture is the message.
chevron counter, 3 up1.27ptRETINAPerpendicular, not horizontal: pitch 3.50 projects to 2.77 across a 1.50 stroke. At 1x the three chevrons fuse. The @1x rep ships TWO chevrons at pitch 4.50, which projects to 3.51 and leaves a true 2.01pt counter.
closed eye, asleep0.90pt1x LOSSHopeless at 1x and it does not matter: the sleep read is carried by the silhouette (3.40pt tall and 6.53 wide, against 5.07 tall and 5.30 wide awake), not by the eye.
creature interiorn/aPASSThere is no interior. It is one filled mass with two ear notches cut from the outside, which is the only kind of organic detail that survives 16pt.
ear notch1.30pt wideRETINAThe V between the two ears is 1.30pt across at 0.95pt depth. At 1x it half-fills and the creature reads as one lump with a dimple. That is the intended failure: the brief is a filled mass, and a mass with a soft dimple is still a mass.
ghost track1.50pt @ 30%RETINACoincident with the live arc, so it adds no counter. Alpha 0.30 on a 1.50pt stroke is a grey hint at 1x, which is all it is for: it holds the ring's shape when a window is empty so the mark never changes footprint.

7 · What I gave up

  • The dots are not below the ring, they are in its mouth. Stacked under a 16pt ring inside 22pt there is about 3pt left, which is under every floor at once. Opening the arcs to 96° and dropping the dock to y 18.25 lets the dots occupy the same vertical band as the arc terminals instead of a band of their own. It costs 1.75pt of extra ink height (17.50 total, not 16.00) and it is the whole reason the design fits.
  • The drawn ticks became notches. Covered above. This is the single biggest departure from the sketch and I think it is the one improvement.
  • Three dots fit for free; the fourth costs width. Slots 1–3 sit inside the 22pt box. From four agents the mark grows to the right, 28pt at five, 44pt at the ceiling. The menu bar reflows, which is honest but not free — icons to the left of it shift.
  • The creature has no face. Two eyes read as a face at every size tested, so awake it has none at all: the two ear notches and the shoulder taper carry the whole read. Asleep it gets exactly one closed eye, and that eye is 0.90pt and dies at 1×. The sleep state is carried by the silhouette dropping from 5.07pt tall to 3.40 and spreading from 5.30 wide to 6.53.
  • The needs-input state throws the dots out of the mark. When the ring seals there is no mouth to sit in, so the dock steps 16pt to the right and the mark jumps from 28pt to 47pt wide. It is a lurch. I kept it because the alternative — hiding the agents while one is blocked — hides the thing you are about to be asked about.
  • Above ten agents the count is a lie. Nine dots and a pill. Counting ten 2.20pt dots on a 3.50 pitch is not something anyone does at a glance, so past six the dock is texture: how busy, not how many.
