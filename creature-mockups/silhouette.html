1  /  Construction

Every curve in the mark is an arc from a closed vocabulary of three radii — R2, R5, R42 — joined by two straight lines, with tangent continuity at all six junctions. The back and the snout are not eyeballed: the R2 snout circle is internally tangent to the R42 back arc, because the distance between their centres is exactly 40 = 42 − 2. Nothing here was drawn freehand and circled afterwards.

Deliberate exceptions, named. The body sits above the 35–60% coverage band that was measured on square logo marks: at about 76% of its own bounding box it is a solid mass, which is the point of the whole redraw. Against the full 56×22 canvas it is 33%, because the headroom above the ridge is reserved for quills and is load-bearing negative space, not padding. And the element budget of “one silhouette plus one modifier” was derived for a 22×22 square; this strip is two and a half of those, and it spends them on exactly three zones — creature, overrun, rail.

2  /  Silhouette test

Solid black, no fill boundary, no opacity levels, no interior detail whatsoever. If it fails here it fails everywhere — so this is the drawing the whole mark was built backwards from.

22pt · actual size
3× · five agents
3× · idle, smooth back

It passes with quills up: a low, heavy-rumped animal with a spiny ridge and a long snout down on the ground — and, read the other way, a horizontal wedge gauge on a rail. That double reading is the design: the form is generated by the gauge, not by the animal. Cover the snout and the rest still says a vessel filling along a track, which is the instrument test passed.

Idle, with the back smooth, it is honestly closer to a wedge than to an animal — I am showing that rather than hiding it. I take that trade deliberately: idle is the state that should recede, and the creature reading returns the instant a single quill goes up. What is not here in any state: no eye, no eye-slit, no mouth, no leg, no foot, no ear, no tail, no fur, no outline.

The mark is 12pt of body in a 22pt bar where the Wi-Fi fan is about 8pt and the battery about 10.5pt, so it sits fractionally taller and, at 30% vessel opacity, distinctly lighter. It is wide rather than tall, like Itsycal, and takes its weight from length. On the light bar the same alpha reads lighter than it does inverted, which is irradiation doing what it always does; the fix if it bothers anyone is a 3% heavier dark variant, not a redraw.

4  /  State gallery

Each state at actual size on a dark chip, then at 3×. Nothing in the mark ever changes meaning between states: the fill is always the 5-hour window, the rail is always the week, a quill is always one running agent, and the amber wall is always “a human is blocking work”.

Idle · 0 agents
5h 12% · week 40% · nothing running. Smooth back, no quills, no ghost, no dashes. The mark is at its shortest: 13.5pt of ink. Absolute stillness.
1 agent
One quill, rearmost slot, on the high point of the rump where it is most visible. 5h 20%.
5 agents
Quills fill forward from the rump at a fixed 3.5pt pitch. 1–6 map one to one and are countable.
7–9 agents
Six quills, all at 1.25× height. The count is no longer exact and the mark says so by changing register rather than by silently saturating.
10+ agents
Six quills at 1.6×, sheared 11° forward, body leaning 0.5pt into the run. The spine does not arch — the quills already own the headroom.
5h at 85%
The fill has reached the taper. The leading edge is now only 5pt tall, so the last fifth of the window is visually tense by construction, not by colour.
Burn overrun — real data
28% eaten, 92%/h burn. Ghost pinned at the snout, three dashes past the nose: exhaustion lands 1h25m before the 3:40am reset. Five agents up, week at 18%.
7-day binding
Week 88% spent. The rail stops short in a cliff, the ghost rail is deleted entirely, and the creature crouches to 0.82×. Height above ground is the week’s headroom.
Needs input ×1
The rail stands up in front of the snout. Same 1.5pt weight, connected to the ground, amber. One blocked session = 4pt of wall. No face, and the gauge keeps reading.
Needs input ×2
Two blocked = 7.5pt, three or more = 11pt. Height counts; nothing pairs; there is no configuration of this mark that resolves into a face.
Stale data
Nothing anywhere at full opacity — vessel 38%, rail 38% along its whole length, no fill, no quills. Full black anywhere means the reading is live, so its absence is unambiguous.

5  /  Live scenario — 18s loop

Idle, work starting, a fan-out to five, one agent finishing, the burn projection going bad, a session blocking, and the block clearing. Every timing below is the shipped one. Idle motion is exactly zero: between events nothing on this page moves, and the last three seconds of the loop are perfectly still because that stillness is the completion signal.

The one change to the motion budget: the 60-second blink used to be an eye. There is no eye, so it is a 120ms alpha dip on the amber wall — same cadence, same hard stop at ten minutes, applied to the element that is actually asking for something. The 220ms front-view morph is gone entirely, and with it the worst property of the old design: for as long as you were blocked, the morph destroyed all four other readings. The wall costs one 1.5pt rectangle and destroys nothing.

6  /  What changed, and what is still weak

Deleted

The face, in every state. The eye-slit. The front-view morph. The two-eye blocked state. The numeral — it was the only element in the mark whose meaning changed between states, so the fix was to remove it rather than relabel it; the exact percentage lives in the panel that drops down, one click away. Also gone: legs, feet, tail, and the outlined contour.

Redrawn

The body is now a rump-high, snout-low wedge instead of a generic quadruped, so the gauge is the animal. Three radii replace an open curve vocabulary. The fill boundary is a hard vertical between a 100% and a 30% plateau instead of a soft edge inside a dark body, which is what makes 28% and 34% differ by a visible 2.0pt. The dorsal ridge slopes, so the fill’s leading edge also shortens as it advances — two cues for one number.

The face problem, solved

Blocked is no longer an expression, it is an obstruction: the ground rail turns up in front of the snout. Same 1.5pt weight, structurally connected to the rail, amber. Height counts the blocked sessions — 4pt, 7.5pt, 11pt. Nothing pairs, nothing is round, nothing sits above a curve. It also survives template rendering with colour stripped, because the shape carries the state and the amber only raises it.

Still weak — stated, not hidden

Exact agent counts above six are gone and cannot be recovered from a 22pt mark; three legible tiers is what the counter floor affords. The wall and the overrun dashes share a vertical/horizontal vocabulary and sit 2pt apart, which is the one place a glance could misread. And with the numeral deleted, precision now costs a click.
