The drawing, undiluted

Two bottom-open remaining-quota arcs, a 50% tick on each at top dead centre, a creature in the middle that only moves while agents run, agent dots in the bottom gap. Built at 22×22pt, 1pt = 1px, nothing removed from the sketch. The bill for that arrives in the rasterisation note at the bottom.

1 · Construction · 10×

All coordinates in points, origin at the top-left of the 22×22 box. Ring centre (11.00, 10.75). Every stroke edge lands on a 0.50pt boundary, so every edge is a whole device pixel at 2×.

2 · Silhouette test · no colour at all

Left pair is the true silhouette — every element at alpha 1.0, which is what a pure black template would be. Right pair is what actually ships: alpha grading is the only channel a template image keeps, so the 7-day arc sits at 0.86 and the empty track at 0.20 to build hierarchy without colour.

Flat silhouette · alpha 1.0 everywhere
Every element forced to alpha 1.0 — the hardest version of the test. Both arcs, both spurs, the creature and all five dots stay separate.
Shipping template · alpha grading only, on a dark bar
outer arc 1.00 · inner arc 0.86 · empty track 0.20 · creature and dots 1.00. Alpha is the only hierarchy channel a template image keeps.

Verdict: the composition survives monochrome. What it loses is the identity of the two arcs — without green and white, 5-hour and 7-day are told apart only by radius and by 0.86 vs 1.0 alpha. That is enough to read “two nested gauges” but not enough to know which is which without learning it once.

6 · Rasterisation note · the honest bill

Floors used: stroke ≥ 1.0pt; counter ≥ 2.0pt at 1×, ≥ 1.0pt at 2×. Every edge in this mark is on a 0.50pt grid, so at 2× every edge lands on a whole device pixel. At 1× a 1.50pt stroke is mathematically incapable of landing both edges on whole pixels — each arc is one solid row plus one half-covered row. That is unavoidable and it is not the problem. The counters are the problem.

What a non-Retina Mac actually sees

  • One thick gauge band over the top whose swept length is the remaining quota. Two windows become one.
  • A dark rounded mass in the middle that grows and shrinks when work is running.
  • A dashed bar under it that gets longer with more agents.
  • A red circle with a solid arrowhead when something is waiting.
  • A low mound with the gauge empty when the quota is gone.

That is still a working icon. It is just not this icon.

What I refused to trade

  • Ink height. 18.50pt in a 22pt box, against the 16pt convention. A bottom-open ring with a dot row under it cannot be 16pt and still hold five bands. The mark reads heavier than Tailscale’s.
  • The 1.00pt counter between arcs. Widening it to the 2.00pt 1× floor would have left the creature 2.0pt tall. The creature was not negotiable, so the counter took the hit.
  • The ticks touch their arcs. A floating tick needs 1.0 + 1.0 + 1.0 = 3.00pt of clear band and there is no 3.00pt band anywhere in this design. Making them spurs off the arc is the only version that fits; it is the single largest deviation from the sketch.
